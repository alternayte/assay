title: ADR-001 Use PostgreSQL for job queues labels: adr author: alex.acme created: 2026-01-15T09:00:00Z updated: 2026-01-20T09:00:00Z ---

Status

AcceptedGreen

Decision

The reminder service uses PostgreSQL for its job queue. It does not use a separate message broker.

Rationale

One datastore is easier to operate. The queue load is below 50 jobs per second.
