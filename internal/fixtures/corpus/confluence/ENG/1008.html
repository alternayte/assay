Terms

TermDefinition
ManuscriptA paper that an author sends to a journal for review.
Article Processing ChargeThe fee that an author pays when a journal accepts a paper.
EditorThe person who decides whether a journal accepts a manuscript.

Authors pay the Article Processing Charge (APC) after acceptance.
